MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT (SAMPLE — FICTIONAL PARTIES)

Effective date: January 15, 2026. Parties: Bluefin Robotics Inc. and Oakridge Medical Devices LLC.

1. Purpose. The parties wish to evaluate a possible joint development of a surgical robotics component.
2. Confidential information. Each party shall keep the other party's confidential information secret and use it only for the Purpose.
3. Duration. Obligations survive for three (3) years after the effective date.
4. Disputes. Any dispute shall be resolved by binding arbitration in New York, NY.
