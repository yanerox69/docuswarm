SAAS SUBSCRIPTION AGREEMENT (SAMPLE — FICTIONAL PARTIES)

This Agreement is entered into on March 1, 2026 between Northwind Analytics Ltd. ("Provider") and Harbor Logistics S.A. ("Customer").

1. Term. The initial term is twelve (12) months. This Agreement will auto-renew for successive twelve-month periods unless either party gives written notice at least ninety (90) days before the end of the then-current term.
2. Fees. Customer shall pay USD 48,000 per year, invoiced annually in advance.
3. Termination. Provider may terminate immediately if Customer fails to pay within fifteen (15) days of the due date. Early termination by Customer triggers a penalty equal to the fees remaining in the current term.
4. Governing law. This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware.
