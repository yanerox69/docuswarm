EXCLUSIVE DISTRIBUTION AGREEMENT (SAMPLE — FICTIONAL PARTIES)

Supplier: Andes Coffee Exporters S.A.C. Distributor: Maple Leaf Foods Distribution Inc. Signed on April 20, 2026.

1. Appointment. Supplier appoints Distributor as its exclusive distributor in Canada. Supplier shall not sell directly or through third parties in the territory.
2. Minimum purchase. Distributor shall purchase at least 40 metric tons per year. Failure to meet the minimum allows Supplier to terminate the exclusivity on thirty (30) days' notice.
3. Non-compete. During the term and for two (2) years after, Distributor shall not distribute competing coffee brands in Canada.
4. Governing law. Laws of the Province of Ontario.
