COMMERCIAL OFFICE LEASE (SAMPLE — FICTIONAL PARTIES)

Landlord: Cedar Point Properties LLC. Tenant: Lumen Design Studio Inc. Premises: Suite 410, 200 Market Street.

1. Term. Five (5) years commencing June 1, 2026 and ending May 31, 2031.
2. Rent. USD 9,500 per month, increasing 3% each anniversary.
3. Default. If Tenant vacates before the end of the term, Tenant shall pay liquidated damages equal to six (6) months of rent.
4. Indemnification. Tenant shall indemnify Landlord against all claims arising from Tenant's use of the Premises, including claims caused by Landlord's negligence.
5. Renewal. Tenant has one option to extend for five years at market rent, exercisable by written notice no later than November 30, 2030.
