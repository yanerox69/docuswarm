TERM LOAN AGREEMENT (SAMPLE — FICTIONAL PARTIES)

Lender: Granite Bay Capital Partners. Borrower: Solstice Solar Installers Inc. Closing date: May 5, 2026.

1. Principal. USD 2,000,000, repayable in 36 monthly installments beginning June 5, 2026.
2. Interest. 11.5% per annum, fixed.
3. Prepayment. Any prepayment during the first 24 months is subject to a penalty of 4% of the amount prepaid.
4. Events of default. Lender may terminate the commitment and accelerate all amounts if Borrower's cash balance falls below USD 250,000 at any month end.
5. Guarantee. The founders personally indemnify Lender for all losses under this Agreement.
